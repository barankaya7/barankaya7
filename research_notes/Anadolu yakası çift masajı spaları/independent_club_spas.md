# Couples massage (çift masajı) at non-hotel day spas and club spas, Anatolian side of Istanbul

Research date: 2026-11-04. Method limits: only WebSearch worked. WebFetch could not reach grupanya.com, firsatbufirsat.com or mesprestiges.com (DNS blocked in this session), so all details below come from search-result summaries, not full pages. **No Google Maps rating or review count was verified for any venue.** Anything not cited is listed under Gaps.

## Which reputable non-hotel spas / club spas on the Asian side offer couples massage, with what prices, durations and packages?

### Takeaway
Only a few non-hotel venues have a documented couples option: **Dream Day Spa (Bağdat Cad., Kadıköy)**, with a dated, priced couples deal (3,299 TL for 50 min, valid to 20 Jan 2027), **EsteSpa Suadiye** (day spa with sauna and jacuzzi, couples option unconfirmed), and **So'u Spa & Wellness inside Shaba club at Akasya (Acıbadem/Üsküdar)** (proper club spa with hamam services, couples option unconfirmed). Most other "Anadolu yakası çift masajı" hits are SEO-driven massage-salon sites. Treat those with caution.

### Cited Findings

**1. Dream Day Spa Center, Bağdat Caddesi (Caddebostan/Erenköy, Kadıköy)**
- Current Grupanya deal: couples 50-minute massage at 3,299 TL, down from 4,500 TL (27% off), valid until 20 Jan 2027, for men and women. The same listing has a single 50-min Bali or anti-stress massage at 1,800 TL and a 30-min head/back/neck massage at 1,399 TL. — [Grupanya – Dream Day Spa](https://www.grupanya.com/dream-day-spa/dream-day-spa-center-masaj)
- Earlier deal: couples 50 min at 2,500 TL (list 3,200 TL), valid to 23 Jan 2026, which shows prices rose about 30% within a year. — [Grupanya](https://www.grupanya.com/firsat/dream-day-spa-center-masaj)
- Open every day 11:00–20:00. Appointment required. — [Grupanya mobile](https://m.grupanya.com/istanbul/dreamday-60dakika)
- Address differs between listings: Bağdat Cad. No:325 (Erenköy) in one and No:293 (Caddebostan) in another. Confirm by phone. — [Grupanya](https://www.grupanya.com/dream-day-spa)
- Ratings on the deal site: 4.6 from 35 reviews on one page and 4.4 from 93 ratings on another. Visible comments praise the "experienced masseuses", and a 2025 comment calls it "affordable and clean". These are Grupanya deal-buyer ratings, not Google. — [Grupanya](https://www.grupanya.com/dream-day-spa)
- Hamam appears in its general service list, but no couples hamam package was found. — [Grupanya](https://www.grupanya.com/istanbul/firsat/dream-day-spa-center-masaj)
- The venue has run deals for many years, with very old listings at 84 TL and 28 TL, so it is long-established. — [Grupanya old deal](https://www.grupanya.com/istanbul/firsat/dream-dayspa-1kisilikbali); [FırsatOn](https://www.firsaton.com/firsat/bagdat-caddesi-dream-day-spa-center-da-hint-ya-da-refleksoloji-masaji-60-tl-yerine-28-tl-211672)

**2. EsteSpa Suadiye (Bağdat Caddesi 374/6, Suadiye, Kadıköy)**
- Day spa offering massage, sauna, jacuzzi and professional skincare. Open Tue–Sun 10:00–21:00. Phone (0216) 411 12 63-64. Website estespa.com. — [EsteSpa official](http://www.estespa.com/); [Mes Prestiges guide (2026)](https://mesprestiges.com/en/istanbul/places/estespa-suadiye)
- Third-party guide puts average spend at about 3,000 TRY per person (range 1,500–6,000 TRY) and says package deals exist. Reservations are by phone or Instagram. The May 2026 and Dec 2024 versions of the guide show the same numbers, so they may be stale. No couples-specific price was found. — [Mes Prestiges](https://mesprestiges.com/en/istanbul/places/estespa-suadiye)

**3. So'u Spa & Wellness, inside Shaba Health & Fitness / Life Club, next to Akasya AVM (Acıbadem, Üsküdar)**
- Located "Akasya AVM yanı, Shaba Life Club'da, 34718 Üsküdar". Offers classic, deep tissue, Bali, reflexology and aromatherapy massages plus traditional Turkish hamam treatments. — [Spafoni](https://www.spafoni.com/tr/uskudar-acibadem-akasya-so-u-spa-wellness-da-masaj-keyfi-ve-spa-kullanimi); [Fırsat Bu Fırsat](https://www.firsatbufirsat.com/firsat/acibadem-akasya-sou-spa-wellness-indirim)
- Spafoni deal: 50-min basic massage (weekdays until 15:00) at 1,600 TRY (list 1,950), with spa-area use. Expired Fırsat Bu Fırsat deals: a 5-massage package at 9,999 TL (list 12,600) and a classic Swedish massage with kese-köpük at 1,299 TL. — [Spafoni](https://www.spafoni.com/tr/uskudar-acibadem-akasya-so-u-spa-wellness-da-masaj-keyfi-ve-spa-kullanimi); [FBF 5-pack](https://www.firsatbufirsat.com/firsat/sou-spa-wellness-masaj-paketi)
- Foursquare: 8.0/10 from 27 ratings. One reviewer praised customer communication and the "high level" of the therapists. A deal-page review from about 2024 calls it "clean and nice, very successful masseuse". — [Foursquare](https://foursquare.com/v/sou-spa/59a3c79ae185f358f6b4dae6)
- Listed phone: +90 549 599 00 45 (unverified). — search summary of [FBF venue page](https://www.firsatbufirsat.com/mekan/shaba-akasya-avm)
- No couples room or couples price was found.

**4. Spaceday Spa (Suadiye and Erenköy, Bağdat Cad.)**
- Had a "Couple Room" at its Erenköy branch, but the source is about 15 years old. Current status unknown. — [Spaceday blog](https://spacespa.wordpress.com/)

**5. Lower-confidence venues (SEO "masaj salonu" sites; treat cautiously)**
- Çikolata Spa Kalamış (Kadıköy): its own site claims private couples rooms for side-by-side massage, 50-min sessions, and sauna/jacuzzi. No price is published. The only "reviews" come from its own content and an affiliated-style guide. — [Çikolata Spa](https://www.cikolataspamerkezleri.com/ciftlere-ozel-spa-ve-masaj-hizmeti/); [haydibutikmasaj guide](https://www.haydibutikmasaj.com/kadikoy-masaj-rehberi-en-populer-masaj-salonlari-ve-spa-merkezleri/)
- Nina Spa (Şerifali, Ataşehir/Ümraniye border): advertises a couples cabin. Its only review is self-quoted on its own site. — [Nina Spa](https://ninaspamerkezi.com/)
- Search results also turned up sites with explicit "mutlu son" wording (e.g., atasehir.vipmasajspa.com). These are red-flag adult-service sites and are excluded. — [example](https://atasehir.vipmasajspa.com/)

**Hotel-based (out of scope, noted only for price context)**
- Bali Asia Spa at Tryp by Wyndham Ataşehir: 2-person 50-min VIP room with private sauna, jacuzzi, fruit plate and drink for 4,899 TL (deal site). One reviewer: massage very good, but the room was small and cooled only by a fan. — [Fırsat Bu Fırsat](https://www.firsatbufirsat.com/firsat/bali-asia-spa-tryp-by-wyndham-atasehir-masaj)
- Generic 2026 Istanbul benchmark: 60-min couples massage at about ₺4,500 (one generic price guide). — [masajrehberiavrupa](https://masajrehberiavrupa.net/masaj-fiyatlari-2026)

### Inferences
- Couples massage at a mid-to-good Kadıköy day spa costs roughly 3,300–4,500 TL for 50–60 min in late 2026 (Dream Day Spa deal vs. list price). A hotel VIP couples room with private sauna/jacuzzi costs about 4,900 TL.
- So'u Spa (Shaba/Akasya) is the closest match to a "premium club spa with hamam" on the Asian side. Deal pages say spa-area use comes with the massage, so membership is apparently not required (inferred, unconfirmed).
- Dream Day Spa is the most clearly documented couples offer at a standalone venue. Grupanya buyer ratings in the mid-4s are a moderate quality signal, not a premium one.

### Gaps
- No Google Maps or Yandex ratings or review counts could be verified for any venue. Full reviews (Google, Yandex, TripAdvisor, Şikayetvar) could not be read because page fetching was blocked.
- Couples-room availability and pricing at EsteSpa and So'u Spa are unconfirmed. Ask by phone or Instagram.
- Instagram handles were not found or verified for any venue.
- Mall spas at Palladium Ataşehir, Brandium, Optimum, Emaar Square and Viaport Asia: no couples-massage spa was found in searches. (Vialand is on the European side.)
- Branded chains (Elite Spa, Tara Spa, Sultan's Spa, etc.) with Asian-side non-hotel branches were not found in searches.
- Facility details (hamam, steam, pool, relaxation lounge) beyond those listed above are unverified.

## Premium fitness-club spas on the Asian side (Mars/MAC, Hillside, Sports International, Shaba, etc.)

### Takeaway
No evidence was found of a couples massage offer at MACFit/MAC, Hillside or Sports International on the Asian side. Mars Athletic's spa brand is NuSpa, which is excluded. Hillside's spa (Sanda Day Spa) is at İstinye, on the European side. The best-documented club spa on the Asian side is So'u Spa in the Shaba club at Akasya (see above).

### Cited Findings
- Mars Athletic runs the MAC, MACFit and NuSpa brands. — [Kariyer.net job listing](https://www.kariyer.net/is-ilani/mars-spor-kulubu-ve-tesisleri-isletmeciligi-anonim-personal-coach-4194116)
- Time Out says Mars Athletic Club locations were rebranded as MACFit, and mentions ZONE clubs at Ataşehir Brandium and on Bağdat Caddesi. No massage service is mentioned. — [Time Out Istanbul](https://www.timeout.com/istanbul/tr/spor-ve-fitness/hangi-spor-salonuna-neden-gitmeli)
- Hillside City Club "Trio" branch is described as serving people on the Anatolian side (gym and pool). Spa and massage are not mentioned. Sanda Day Spa (60 treatments, hamam, sauna, steam, hydropool, spa lounge) is at Hillside İstinye. Sources are old (around 2009). — [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/istanbul/tr/spor-ve-fitness/hangi-spor-salonuna-neden-gitmeli); [Mynet](https://www.mynet.com/hillside-city-club-istinyede-neler-var-1256-mykadin)
- Sports International: the only spa reference found is a spa-therapist job ad in Ankara (Çankaya). No Ataşehir/Istanbul spa evidence. — [Kariyer.net](https://www.kariyer.net/is-ilani/sports-international-bilkent-fitness-ve-spor-a-s-spa-terapisti-3793315)
- Shaba Health & Fitness Club (Akasya) hosts So'u Spa. — [Yandex Maps – Shaba](https://yandex.com.tr/maps/org/shaba_health_fitness_club/47265799410/)

### Inferences
- If the user wants a "club spa" atmosphere on the Asian side without NuSpa, So'u Spa at Shaba/Akasya is the main candidate found.

### Gaps
- Whether Hillside's Asian-side branch currently has a spa, and whether "Trio" still operates, is unverified.
- Fitness Flex spa offerings were not found.

## McFit / John Reed / MACFit partner spas and member discounts on couples massage

### Takeaway
The only documented spa perk is MACFit's "NuSpa'da %40 indirim" (Classic/Gold). No McFit or John Reed partner spa other than NuSpa, and no member discount valid for couples massage, was found.

### Cited Findings
- MACFit packages: Classic and Gold include a 40% discount at NUSPA, used by showing the eligible membership at the branch. Gold also includes 25% off MACFit personal training and 2 guest passes per month. — [MACFit Üyelik Paketleri](https://www.macfit.com/uyelik-paketleri)
- Oct 2026 campaign: 50% off the first month for Maximum Card holders, valid for membership only, not spa services. — [MACFit kampanya](https://www.macfit.com/kampanyalar/macfit-uyeliginde-yuzde-50-maximum-kart-avantaji)
- McFit and John Reed are RSG Group brands. — [Wikipedia – RSG Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RSG_Group)
- A forum post says sauna and massage may be offered as add-ons at John Reed. Not an official source. — [forum](https://hisardepolama.com/threads/john-reed-fitness-fiyat.21665/)

### Inferences
- The user's "McFit Gold" is probably MACFit Gold, but this is not certain. Its spa perk is limited to NuSpa, and the user reports that perk excludes couples massage. Couples savings will more likely come from deal sites (Grupanya, Spafoni, Fırsat Bu Fırsat) than from gym membership.

### Gaps
- No official John Reed Turkey benefits page was found. No partner-spa list for McFit, John Reed or MACFit beyond NuSpa was found.
