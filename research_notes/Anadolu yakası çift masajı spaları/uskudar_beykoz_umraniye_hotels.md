# Couples massage (çift masajı) at hotel spas: Üsküdar, Beykoz, Bosphorus Asian shore, Ümraniye, Çekmeköy, Sancaktepe

Research method note (read first): this session's network blocks direct page fetches. Fetches of eliteworldhotels.com.tr, sumahan.com, expedia.com and expedia.com.my all failed with a DNS/allow-list error, so no official spa menus, PDF price lists, Google Maps pages or full review texts could be opened. Everything below comes from web-search result snippets, mostly booking aggregators (Expedia, Hotels.com, Jollytur, Setur, Ucuzabilet, Obilet, Wanderlog, Reischeck). **No couples-massage price in TL (2025–2026) was found for any venue, and no Google Maps spa rating was found.** Treat every venue as "needs a phone or WhatsApp check" before anyone recommends it. Nu Spa is excluded as instructed.

## Which hotel spas in the target districts offer couples massage or couples rooms?

### Takeaway
Two hotels have aggregator listings that name couples treatment rooms: **Crowne Plaza Istanbul – Oryapark (Ümraniye), with SPA GOA**, and **Limak Eurasia Luxury Hotel (Beykoz/Kavacık side)**. Elite World Village Polonezköy, DoubleTree by Hilton Ümraniye, Tryp by Wyndham Sancaktepe and INVENTIST Hotel (Çekmeköy) all have spas, but no listing confirms couples massage. For Sumahan on the Water (Çengelköy), no spa or couples treatment was confirmed in the snippets.

- The spa, "SPA GOA", has 5 treatment rooms, **including rooms for couples** — [Expedia](https://expedia.ie/Istanbul-Hotels-Crowne-Plaza-Istanbul-Oryapark.h6057596.Hotel-Information) / [Hotels.com](https://es.hotels.com/ho462701) (search snippet)

Spa services are marked "*" as charged extra — [Setur](https://www.setur.com.tr/crowne-plaza-istanbul-oryapark-by-ihg); [Tatilbudur](https://www.tatilbudur.com/crowne-plaza-istanbul-oryapark)

### Inferences
- The strongest candidates in scope for a hotel couples massage are **Limak Eurasia** (big spa, couples rooms listed, free hamam/sauna/steam for hotel guests) and **Crowne Plaza Oryapark SPA GOA** (couples rooms listed, hamam and steam room). Both look like real chain or luxury spas, not small massage parlours.
- Under the Jollytur policy, hamam, sauna and steam are free for Limak Eurasia hotel guests. That policy may not cover day guests, so it needs checking.
- The Ritz-Carlton Istanbul is on the European side (Şişli), as far as I know (not confirmed by a source here). Treat it as out of scope.
- A'jia Hotel (Kanlıca), Beykoz Kundura-area hotels, Çamlıca-area hotels, Ramada/Wyndham Ümraniye, Holiday Inn Ümraniye, and Hyatt/Marriott properties in these districts produced no spa or couples-massage evidence in my searches. Whether they have a spa is unknown, not confirmed absent.

### Gaps

- **Day guests:** not confirmed for any venue.
- **Google Maps, TripAdvisor, Yandex:** no spa-specific rating, review count or dated review quotes were gathered. The ratings above are hotel-level only, from booking aggregators.
- **Instagram, phone, WhatsApp:** only the DoubleTree phone (+90 216 613 0000) and the Crowne Plaza address were found.
- **Not checked at all:** A'jia Hotel, Kundura area, Çamlıca hotels, Ramada/Wyndham/Holiday Inn Ümraniye, Hyatt/Marriott.

## What are the facilities, prices and review sentiment per venue?

### Takeaway
Facility data comes from aggregators and is reasonably consistent. Price and review-sentiment data is essentially missing because pages could not be fetched. The only spa-specific review sentiment found is positive, for Limak Eurasia: clean spa, good massages, wide menu.

### Cited Findings
- Limak Eurasia: about 800 m² spa, hamam, sauna, steam, indoor pool. Massages rated good; menu includes traditional Turkish treatments, Bali massage and hot stone — [Reischeck](https://www.reischeck.nl/hotels/limak-eurasia-luxury-hotel/)
- Limak Eurasia: hamam, sauna and steam free (for guests), massage charged — [Jollytur](https://www.jollytur.com/limak-eurasia-luxury)
- Crowne Plaza Oryapark: hamam and steam room, spa services charged extra — [Setur](https://www.setur.com.tr/crowne-plaza-istanbul-oryapark-by-ihg)
- Tryp Sancaktepe: warning that spa services may be temporarily unavailable — [Ucuzabilet](https://www.ucuzabilet.com/otel/Tryp-By-Wyndham-Istanbul-Sancaktepe)
- Caution on misattributed reviews: positive Etstur reviews about a mixed-gender hamam and Bali massage belong to **Limak Yalova Thermal**, not Limak Eurasia — [Etstur](https://www.etstur.com/Limak-Yalova-Thermal-Boutique)

### Inferences
- Before recommending a venue, a caller should ask for: the couples-massage price, whether the price includes hamam, sauna or pool access, whether non-guests can book, and whether a couples room (not two separate rooms) is guaranteed.

### Gaps
- No upselling, noise, waiting-lounge or red-flag review data for any venue.
- No Şikayetvar, Ekşi Sözlük or deal-site (Grupanya, Şehirfırsatı, Hotelistan) entries were retrieved.
