# Couples massage (çift masajı) at hotel spas: Kadıköy / Kozyatağı / Ataşehir / Kalamış-Fenerbahçe-Moda (research date 2026-11-04)

**Method note (read first):** This session could not open pages directly. WebFetch failed with DNS/allowlist errors on marriott.com, radissonhotels.com, grupanya.com and firsatbufirsat.com. Everything below comes from search-engine result snippets and summaries of the cited pages, not from reading the full pages. Google Maps ratings and review counts could not be retrieved for any venue.** Nu Spa is excluded as instructed.

## Which chain or 5-star hotels in scope have a spa with a couples room?

### Takeaway
Couples rooms are confirmed or reported at Hilton Kozyatağı (HitClub/Hitspa, couples suite on Hilton's official page), Wyndham Grand Kalamış (Blue Harmony Spa), Radisson Blu Asia, Marriott Asia (Palestra Spa), Sheraton Grand Ataşehir, Park Inn by Radisson Ataşehir, Selectum City Ataşehir (Shiva Spa), Atacity Hotel (Zen Spa) and A11 Hotel Ataşehir (Sense Spa). Only Hilton's couples suite comes from an official hotel page. The rest come from OTA amenity lists or deal sites. DoubleTree Moda has a spa (reportedly "Qualia") with a hamam, but I found no evidence of a couples room there.

### Inferences
- Recent couples prices on deal sites for mid-tier Ataşehir hotel spas (Atacity, Selectum/Shiva, A11) run about **3,300–5,200 TL for 2×50 minutes plus wet area**, and **7,000–7,500 TL with kese-köpük or a VIP room**. Chain 5-star hotels (Hilton, Wyndham Grand, Marriott, Radisson Blu, Sheraton) are likely to be the same or higher at rack rate. That is an estimate, not a verified price. Hilton single 50-minute packages at about 3,450–3,550 TL would put a couples session at around 7,000 TL.
- The strongest verified chain options for a couples suite with a hamam and wet area are Hilton Kozyatağı (official) and Wyndham Grand Kalamış (Blue Harmony, sea-front Kalamış). The mid-tier options are bookable by day guests through deal sites.
- Shiva Spa (Selectum) shows a gap between near-perfect platform ratings (4.93) and specific Şikayetvar complaints. Treat the high rating with caution.
