## 1. Which hotel spas in these districts actually offer couples massage / couples rooms?

### Takeaway
The best-documented couples option is **Shiva Spa at Hampton by Hilton Pendik**. It has an explicit "same-room 50-min couples massage + 2 h wet area" package on deal sites (3,900–6,200 TL depending on source and date). Several chain hotels are listed by OTAs as having couples treatment rooms but publish no couples price: Radisson Blu Tuzla (Elysia Spa), DoubleTree by Hilton Tuzla, La Quinta by Wyndham Kartal, Titanic Business Kartal (BeFine Spa) and Nearport Sabiha Gökçen. The OTA claims are unverified. Many brands named in the brief turned up nothing in these districts: Hilton Kartal, Wyndham Grand Kartal, Radisson Kartal, Novotel/Mercure Tuzla, Sheraton, Crowne Plaza, Courtyard, Point, Divan, Dedeman. Nothing was found for Sultanbeyli either.

- Hampton by Hilton Pendik (Shiva Spa) is the only hotel spa in scope with a publicly priced couples package. - Non-hotel guests can clearly book at Hampton/Shiva, Green Park Aqua Plus and Holiday Inn Tuzla Bay, since they sell public deals. Radisson Blu Tuzla historically sold non-guest day entry. For the others, outside-guest access is unverified.

## 2. Facilities, inclusions and booking conditions

### Takeaway
At most venues massage is a paid extra and wet-area use (sauna/hamam/steam) comes bundled with deal-site massage packages. The Hampton/Shiva couples package explicitly includes 2 h of wet area (pool, sauna, hamam).

### Cited Findings
- Hampton/Shiva: the couples package includes "2 Saat Islak Alan Kullanımı" (2 h wet-area use), and the deal title says pool + wet area are included. — [Grupanya](https://m.grupanya.com/istanbul/firsat/hampton-by-hilton-pendik-poseidon-spa-masaj); [FBF](https://www.firsatbufirsat.com/firsat/hampton-by-hilton-poseidon-spa-masaj)
- Green Park Pendik: only wet-area use is free for spa guests. Massage, kese and foam are extra. — [Otelz](https://www.otelz.com/hotel/the-green-park-pendik)
- Radisson Blu Tuzla: spa reservation is required, with massage slots 08:00–21:00. Hotel guests get complimentary spa-centre access. — [Booking.com](https://booking.com/hotel/tr/radisson-blu-amp-spa-istanbul-tuzla.html)
- DoubleTree Tuzla: spa area, sauna and hamam are paid. — [weg.de](https://www.weg.de/hotel/tuerkei/istanbul/doubletree-by-hilton-hotel-istanbul-tuzla_hid-2391795)

### Inferences
- For a couples day-visit with hamam, Hampton/Shiva (deal) or Radisson Blu Tuzla (direct, likely higher price) are the realistic choices. Ask whether kese-köpük can be added.

- No relaxation-lounge descriptions were found for any venue. Jacuzzi presence is confirmed only via OTA lists (DoubleTree hot tub, Titanic hot tubs, Elite Dragos jacuzzi).
